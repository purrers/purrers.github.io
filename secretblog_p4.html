M'S CHAI COFFEE REVIEWS


Meerkat Cafè Coffee

Today I'm doing something I've never done before and probably will never do again. I'm reviewing a coffee. I went on an excursion with my Tafe class to Melbourne Zoo yesterday. The chai mix they were using at the Meerkat Cafè was not vegan (it contained milk powder) so I ordered a soy latte.
Flavour: I don't like coffee. 1/5
Price: $6.35? get outta here 1/5
Experience: I overheard the barista say "How am I supposed to make a coffee in this!?" - 'this' being my 500ml insulated drink bottle. Well, I'll tell you how. You get a shot glass, you use the shot glass to catch the coffee coming out of the machine, then you pour the coffee from the shot glass into the bottle. Deadass, it's not that hard. The most skilled barista I ever worked with in my hospitality days had a regular customer who always gave her a drink bottle to put his coffee in. Did she complain about it? No. She did latte art in it. Goddamn. 0/5. Zoo though 5/5. Check out the pics I took!
