Now, a lot of people will tell you that uBlock Origin stopped working ages ago. I’m sure that’s true for most people, but most people are not still using Chrome on a 2012 MacBook Pro that runs Catalina.

I promise that uBlock Origin was still working for me without a hitch until maybe a month ago. Then it started intermittently letting some ads in, but, resetting the uBlock extension made it work just fine again

Yesterday, on youtube, I saw some ads. I reset the extension, but I was still seeing them. I saw an ad for booking.com featuring The Muppets. I never thought I could be angry at Miss Piggy, but I was, just a little bit.

I thought “well, I guess it’s finally time for a new laptop” but then I thought “surely some legend is making a youtube ad blocker that can be used with my ancient OS”

Would you believe it but it turns out that legend is…uBlock origin, but only if your browser is Mozilla Firefox. I had no idea Firefox still existed, like, I had to re-read the reddit post where I found this information a couple of times before I remembered what the hell Firefox even is.

So now on my newly installed Firefox browser with uBlock extension I’m gonna go watch some brain rot. Ciao Ciao

Meerkat Cafè Coffee

Today I'm doing something I've never done before and probably will never do again. I'm reviewing a coffee. I went on an excursion with my Tafe class to Melbourne Zoo yesterday. The chai mix they were using at the Meerkat Cafè was not vegan (it contained milk powder) so I ordered a soy latte.
Flavour: I don't like coffee. 1/5
Price: $6.35? get outta here 1/5
Experience: I overheard the barista say "How am I supposed to make a coffee in this!?" - 'this' being my 500ml insulated drink bottle. Well, I'll tell you how. You get a shot glass, you use the shot glass to catch the coffee coming out of the machine, then you pour the coffee from the shot glass into the bottle. Deadass, it's not that hard. The most skilled barista I ever worked with in my hospitality days had a regular customer who always gave her a drink bottle to put his coffee in. Did she complain about it? No. She did latte art in it. Goddamn. 0/5. Zoo though 5/5. Check out the pics I took!
